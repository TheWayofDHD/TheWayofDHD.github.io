Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026. This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not run accounts, forms or analytics trackers.

What we store

The only data stored in your browser is your language preference under the localStorage key dhd-lang. It stays on your device and is never sent to a server by this site.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Your IP address is processed by Google when the stylesheet is fetched.
  • TON API / Getgems / Tonviewer — public blockchain data is requested from these services when you open pages with live stats or the collection browser. Your browser talks to them directly.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting. Standard access logs may be retained by GitHub under their policies.
  • Telegram — outbound links to t.me open Telegram; their privacy policy applies once you leave this site.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no email forms, no personal data fields.
  • No cookies set by this site (language is stored in localStorage, not cookies).
  • No analytics scripts (GA4, Plausible, Umami) are installed at this time.
  • No wallet connections on this site — purchases happen on Getgems.

Your choices

You can clear the language preference any time by deleting site data for thewayofdhd.github.io in your browser settings. Blocking third-party fonts will not break the site; a system font stack will be used.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open the contact page and write in the Telegram channel or collectors chat.

This page is provided for transparency. It is not legal advice.
