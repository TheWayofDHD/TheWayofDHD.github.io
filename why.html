Why buy a DHD NFT?

The Way of DHD (Путь ХЧК) is 1022 hand-drawn characters on TON, alive since 30 May 2022. A floor of 1 TON opens the door — and one Diamond, the Dickpaper, holds the management rights of the whole project.

Holder benefits

What you actually get

Art with a face

Every piece is hand-drawn and assembled from 8 trait groups — Type, Background, Legs, Body, Hands, Attribute, Face, Head. No two combinations are alike: 1022 characters, 1022 chapters.

A story you live in

Holders get daily Advent postcards from the bot, the lore of DHD's path and a community that has been building since 2022. The collection is a membership.

The Diamond rule

The single Diamond token — the Dickpaper — states on-chain: buying it transfers the management rights and all the assets of the project to the holder.

FAQ

Quick questions

What do I actually own?

An NFT on the TON blockchain: a hand-drawn DHD character with its traits recorded on-chain. It is yours to hold, sell or gift — the cheapest active lots historically start around 1 TON.

Is $DHD the same as the NFT?

No. $DHD is the project jetton (10,000,000 supply, sold out at the ICO on 23 January 2023). The NFT collection is a separate asset — the two live side by side.

Where do the floor price and holder counts come from?

From the TON blockchain via the public TON API: the cheapest active sale lot is the floor, and the numbers refresh every time you open the page.
