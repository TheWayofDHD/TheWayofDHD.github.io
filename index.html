About

What is The Way of DHD?

DHD is a hero who came into this world with a simple mission: to show the right way. He sees lost and uncertain wanderers and guides them to a brighter future. The collection was first offered to the community on 30 May 2022 and minted on TON.

1022 hand-drawn characters

Every piece is assembled from traits — Type, Background, Legs, Body, Hands, Attribute, Face and Head. No two combinations are identical, so each token is a separate chapter of the same story.

One Diamond — the Dickpaper

The single Diamond token is the Dickpaper: a 1-of-1 artwork whose on-chain description states that its purchase transfers management rights and all assets of the project to the holder.

Built on TON

The collection lives on The Open Network with 658 unique on-chain owners and 249 active listings at the time of the last scan. Trading runs through Getgems, Disintar and other TON marketplaces.

The Dickpaper

The Dickpaper is the only Diamond token of the collection and the heaviest file we host. It is meant to be zoomed: collectors look for hidden clues, jokes and references inside the artwork. The page loads it with a progress indicator and a zoom-and-pan viewer.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is The Way of DHD?

The Way of DHD (Путь ХЧК) is an NFT collection of 1022 hand-drawn characters on the TON blockchain. It was first offered to the community on 30 May 2022 and transferred into holders' wallets in July 2022. The project author is JohnD, the artwork is by DenKa.

How many types are in the collection?

Exactly three types: Epic — 1000 items (97.85% of the supply), Exclusive — 21 items (2.05%), and Diamond — 1 item (0.10%). Rare traits such as Background, Body, Hands, Attribute, Face and Head are combined on top of the type.

What is the Dickpaper?

The Dickpaper is the only Diamond token in the collection — a 1-of-1 artwork. Its on-chain description states: "From DHD with love. The purchase of this token implies the transfer of management rights and all the assets of the project to the holder." The artwork is a full-resolution image made to be zoomed.

How do I buy a DHD NFT?

Open the collection on Getgems and pick any active listing. At the time of the last on-chain scan the cheapest lot was 1 TON and 249 of 1022 items were listed for sale across Getgems, Disintar, Marketapp and other TON marketplaces.

Is $DHD the same as the NFT collection?

No. $DHD is a fungible jetton on TON with a supply of 10,000,000 and the contract EQBCFwW8uFUh-amdRmNY9NyeDEaeDYXd9ggJGsicpqVcHq7B. It is traded on TonRocket, DeDust, TWAPs and Tegro, while the NFTs are traded on marketplaces such as Getgems.

Where does the live data come from?

All numbers on this site are read from the TON blockchain through the public TON API: the collection endpoint gives the supply and owners, and each item's sale field gives the ask price. Floor price is computed as the minimum active ask, so it changes with every new listing.
