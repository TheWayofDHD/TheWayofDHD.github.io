How to buy a DHD NFT on TON

1

Install a TON wallet

Get Tonkeeper (iOS, Android, desktop) or MyTonWallet. Write the 24-word seed phrase on paper and keep it offline — never photograph it, never type it into websites. Nobody from the project will ever ask for it.

2

Top up TON

Buy TON on any major exchange and withdraw it to your wallet address, or top up with a card right inside Tonkeeper. Double-check the network is TON, and keep a small reserve for fees — a transfer costs a fraction of a cent.
