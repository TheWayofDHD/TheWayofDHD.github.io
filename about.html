About The Way of DHD

The Way of DHD (Путь ХЧК) is a hand-drawn NFT collection on the TON blockchain: 1022 characters, first offered to the community on 30 May 2022. Author: JohnD. Artwork: DenKa.

The project

What is The Way of DHD?

DHD is a hero who shows the right way. The collection is three types on-chain: Epic (1000), Exclusive (21 named characters) and a single Diamond — the Dickpaper — which carries the project's management rights.

1022 hand-drawn characters

Every piece is assembled from traits — Type, Background, Legs, Body, Hands, Attribute, Face and Head. No two combinations are identical.

One Diamond — the Dickpaper

The single Diamond token is the Dickpaper: a 1-of-1 artwork whose on-chain description transfers management rights and project assets to the holder.

Built on TON

The collection lives on The Open Network. Trading runs through Getgems and other TON marketplaces. Live stats are read from the public TON API.
